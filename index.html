Why Utrecht?

Utrecht, little amsterdam

Utrecht is a beautiful and lively Dutch city where history and modern life come together. Visit the famous Dom Tower, walk along the unique canals, and explore the historic city centre filled with cosy cafés, restaurants, and shops.

Compared with Amsterdam, Utrecht is smaller and more relaxed, making it easier to explore without the same busy tourist crowds. You can experience the charm of a traditional Dutch city while still enjoying plenty of culture, food, and entertainment. If you want an authentic and more peaceful Dutch experience, Utrecht is the perfect place to visit!

A must do/ see in Utrecht

Muiderslot

Muiden Castle is a castle in the Netherlands, located at the mouth of the Vecht river, some 15 kilometers southeast of Amsterdam, in Muiden, where it flows into what used to be the Zuiderzee.

Address

Herengracht 1, 1398 AA Muiden, Netherlands

What I like about it

Its beautiful medieval architecture and fascinating history. The castle feels like stepping back in time, with its towers, gardens, and old rooms.

Ruby Rose

Ruby Rose is a restaurant in the centre of Utrecht where you can enjoy delicious Mediterranean dishes to share and the finest wines. The restaurant interiour is matching the name, you will see many roses.

Address

Korte Jansstraat 23, 3512 GM Utrecht, Netherlands

What I like about it

If you are a wine lover and looking for a great time with some amazing food, then this is a must! It is also very affordable.

Dom Tower

The Dom Tower of Utrecht is the tallest church tower in the Netherlands, at 112.32 metres in height. It is considered the symbol of Utrecht. The Dom Church, and was built between 1321 and 1382.

Address

Domplein 9, 3512 JE Utrecht, Netherlands

What I like about it

The Dom Tower has its impressive height and beautiful architecture. It is one of Utrecht’s most famous landmarks and offers amazing views over the city.
